What Is Cajun Fried Corn?

Wingstop Cajun Fried Corn consists of short corn-on-the-cob segments cooked and coated with Cajun seasoning. The supplied snapshot lists 5 pieces for $3.99 and 200 calories and 10 pieces for $5.99 and 400 calories.

Does the Larger Order Double the Portion?

Yes. The piece count and listed calories both double. The price rises by $2 rather than doubling, so 10 pieces cost about 60 cents each versus about 80 cents each for the 5-piece order.

Which Order Fits Your Meal?

Five pieces suit one person or a small shared side. Ten pieces work for two or more people. An equal split between two diners gives five pieces and 200 listed calories each.

How Does It Compare With Fries?

The 5-piece corn lists 300 fewer calories than Regular Seasoned Fries. Ten pieces list 100 fewer than Regular fries, although the foods differ in texture, serving style and seasoning coverage.

Can You Change the Seasoning?

Customization depends on the selected store. Check whether the cart allows lighter Cajun seasoning or another adjustment before ordering.

Shared Preparation

Because this is a fried restaurant side, do not assume it avoids shared oil or cross-contact. Review the current Wingstop allergen guide and contact the restaurant when an allergy affects the order.
