Low-Calorie & Diet Tips

  • For lowest calories: Choose bone-in wings with dry rub (Lemon Pepper, Louisiana Rub, Cajun). Skip sauces and creamy dips.
  • Keto-friendly: Plain bone-in wings + dry rub + celery sticks. Avoid sugary sauces (Hawaiian, Mango Habanero, Hot Honey Rub).
  • High-protein option: 10pc bone-in plain wings = ~100g protein, 900 calories.
  • Watch the sauces: Garlic Parmesan adds about 30 calories per wing, Hickory BBQ about 10. Choose hot sauces for flavor with minimal calories.
  • Share large orders: A 20pc boneless with large fries can exceed 3000 calories – split with a friend.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About WingStop Calories

How many calories are in 10 bone-in wings at WingStop? +
10 plain bone-in wings contain about 900 calories. With flavor, 900-1,200 calories depending on the flavor - 10pc Original Hot bone-in wings are about 900 calories.
What is the lowest calorie sauce at WingStop? +
Original Hot and Atomic add no calories over plain - both are 90 calories per wing. Dry rubs add a little: Louisiana Rub adds about 20 calories per wing and Lemon Pepper about 30.
Are WingStop boneless wings healthier than bone-in? +
Boneless wings have more carbs because they're breaded, but per piece they're slightly lower in calories (80-110) than bone-in wings (90-120), with much less protein (4g vs 10g). Bone-in is the better choice for protein and fewer carbs.
How many calories in a WingStop meal deal? +
A typical 10pc combo with fries and a drink ranges from about 1650 to 2350 calories depending on wing type, fries size and drink. Check our calculator above for exact estimates.
Does WingStop have nutrition information available? +
Yes, official WingStop nutrition data is available on their website: wingstop.com/nutrition. Our calculator uses Wingstop's official nutritional guide (April 2025).
