Low-Calorie & Diet Tips

  • For lowest calories: Choose bone-in wings with dry rub (Lemon Pepper, Louisiana Rub, Cajun). Skip sauces and creamy dips.
  • Keto-friendly: Plain bone-in wings + dry rub + celery sticks. Avoid sugary sauces (Hawaiian, Teriyaki, Mango Habanero).
  • High-protein option: 10pc bone-in plain wings = ~50g protein, 900-1100 calories.
  • Watch the sauces: Garlic Parmesan and Honey BBQ add 50-70 calories per wing. Choose hot sauces for flavor with minimal calories.
  • Share large orders: A 20pc boneless with fries can exceed 2500 calories – split with a friend.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About WingStop Calories

How many calories are in 10 bone-in wings at WingStop? +
10 plain bone-in wings contain 800-1200 calories depending on size. With sauce, add 150-500 calories. For reference, 10pc Original Hot bone-in wings are approximately 1150-1350 calories.
What is the lowest calorie sauce at WingStop? +
Original Hot, Mild, and Atomic sauces have the fewest calories (10-20 per serving). Dry rubs like Lemon Pepper and Louisiana Rub add only ~5 calories per wing.
Are WingStop boneless wings healthier than bone-in? +
No – boneless wings have more calories and carbs because they're breaded. A boneless wing (130-180 cal) has about 50-60% more calories than a bone-in wing (80-120 cal). Bone-in is the better choice for lower calories and fewer carbs.
How many calories in a WingStop meal deal? +
A typical 10pc combo with fries and a drink ranges from 1400-2100 calories depending on wing type and sauce. A 15pc combo can reach 2500+ calories. Check our calculator above for exact estimates.
Does WingStop have nutrition information available? +
Yes, official WingStop nutrition data is available on their website: wingstop.com/nutrition. Our calculator is updated with the latest 2026 values.
