Quick Answer: What Comes in a Wingstop Classic Wings Combo?

A Wingstop Classic Wings Combo pairs bone-in wings with a side, dip, and drink. Current official location menus show 6-piece and 10-piece options. Both can be split across up to two flavors and come with regular seasoned fries or veggie sticks, one dip, and a 20-ounce drink. Your selected restaurant’s menu and checkout cart are the final word on availability and price.

How to choose two flavors that work together

Use the two-flavor allowance to create contrast. Pairing two very similar sauces makes the second choice less useful, while a dry rub and a sauced flavor stay distinct across the meal.

  • Bright and savory: Lemon Pepper with Garlic Parmesan.
  • Sweet and spicy: Hickory Smoked BBQ with Mango Habanero.
  • Classic heat: Original Hot with Louisiana Rub.
  • Milder order: Mild with Hawaiian.

Heat tolerance is personal. If Atomic is new to you, avoid assigning the whole order to it. See the flavor comparison for taste and heat notes.

Fries or veggie sticks?

Seasoned fries make the meal heavier and bring another salty component. Veggie sticks provide crunch and a cooler break between sauced wings. Neither choice turns the meal into a fixed nutrition total because the flavor, dip, drink and portion all matter.

Ordering tip: Keep the dip for a hot flavor or choose veggie sticks if you want a fresher contrast. If you want fries, check whether your store offers seasoning or size customizations before checkout.

Calories and nutrition: calculate the full order

A combo is a set of separate choices, not one universal calorie number. Six versus ten wings changes the base. Sauce or rub, fries versus vegetables, ranch or bleu cheese, and the drink can each move the total.

  2. Add the nutrition for every selected flavor.
  3. Add the chosen side and dip.
  4. Add the actual drink, including refills if applicable.

Allergen and cross-contact notes

Do not treat “unbreaded” as an allergen guarantee. Wingstop says food is cooked in shared oil containing soy ingredients. Sauces, seasonings and dips introduce their own allergens, and shared preparation areas create cross-contact risk. Anyone with an allergy should review the current allergen guide and contact the selected restaurant before ordering.

Price and value

There is no reliable national price for this combo. Wingstop pricing changes by restaurant, market and ordering channel; delivery fees, taxes, add-ons and upgrades can also change the final total. Compare the 6-piece and 10-piece price in the same store cart. The larger order is only better value if you want the extra wings.

Frequently asked questions

What comes with a Wingstop Classic Wings Combo?

The official menu currently lists bone-in wings, up to two flavors, regular fries or veggie sticks, one dip and a 20-ounce drink. Local options can differ.

How many flavors can I choose?

The current official 6-piece and 10-piece listings allow up to two flavors.

Are classic wings gluten-free?

Do not assume so. Shared oil and preparation create cross-contact risk, and the selected flavors or dips may contain allergens.

Is the price the same at every Wingstop?

No. Check your chosen store and ordering channel for the live price, taxes, fees and available upgrades.
