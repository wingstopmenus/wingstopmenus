What Is the Wingstop Triple Chocolate Chunk Brownie?

Wingstop serves one brownie square per order. The official product page describes it as a warm, soft, fudgy brownie made with rich cocoa flavor and chocolate chunks throughout. It is sold separately, so a brownie is not automatically included with a wings or tenders combo.

How Much Does It Cost?

The menu page reviewed for this article lists the brownie at $2.49. Treat that as an observed price, not a national guarantee. Restaurant location, taxes, delivery markup, and ordering channel can change the amount in your cart.

What Does the Brownie Taste Like?

Wingstop describes the brownie as rich, warm, soft, and fudgy, with chocolate chunks in each layer. Those are the company's product characteristics, not an independent taste test by this site. If you prefer a dense brownie over a cake-like one, the official description points toward the denser option.

Allergens and Cross-Contact

Do not rely on the product name to judge allergens. Wingstop's allergen declaration warns that cross-contact can occur and advises customers with food allergies to use caution. Review our Wingstop allergen guide and contact the selected restaurant when an allergy affects your order.

Is It a Good Add-On for a Combo?

One brownie is easy to add when you want a separate dessert after a spicy or savory meal. It also adds 430 calories to the order, so include it in the total rather than treating it as part of the listed combo nutrition.
