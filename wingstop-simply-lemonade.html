What Simply Lemonade Size Does Wingstop Sell?

Our August 2026 Wingstop menu snapshot lists a 52 fl oz shareable bottle of Simply Lemonade for an observed $4.99 and 390 calories. It is a sealed multi-serving bottle rather than a fountain drink. Confirm the current bottle, nutrition label and checkout price at your selected restaurant.

How Much Does Each Person Get?

The bottle can be divided in several ways. Four equal pours are 13 oz each; two equal pours are 26 oz each. If the menu’s 390-calorie figure is divided evenly, four portions are about 98 calories each and two portions are about 195 calories each. Those are simple share calculations, not manufacturer serving sizes.

Why Can the Bottle Label and Menu Total Differ?

The Simply brand currently lists a 52 oz package, but package formulas, serving definitions and labels can change. Wingstop’s displayed 390 calories should be read as its current menu figure for the item, while the physical bottle label is the right place to verify the package you actually receive. Do not multiply a remembered serving number without checking how many servings the current label defines.

Is It Better Than Buying Fountain Drinks for a Group?

It depends on group size and preference. The bottle is resealable, avoids melted ice and gives everyone the same lemonade. Fountain drinks allow different flavors and individual cups. At the observed $4.99 price, the bottle costs about $1.25 per person when shared four ways, before tax or fees.

Does It Come With Cups or Ice?

Do not assume cups or ice are included. Restaurant packaging and delivery extras vary, so add any needed items in the cart or request them from the selected store. The bottle can also be chilled and poured at home.

What Should You Check Before Ordering?

  • Make sure the selected store still lists the 52 oz bottle.
  • Check the physical label for the current serving and ingredient information.
  • Confirm whether you need cups or ice.
  • Compare the pickup and delivery totals.
  • Refrigerate after opening according to the package directions.

Nutrition and Allergen Limits

Coca-Cola’s official Simply product page confirms 52 fl oz as an available size. For the item you receive, use the current bottle label. Wingstop also warns that nutrition varies by location and allergen cross-contact cannot be guaranteed.
