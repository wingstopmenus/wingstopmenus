About WingStop - America's Wing Destination

WingStop is a popular American chain of restaurants that specializes in chicken wings. Founded in 1994 in Garland, Texas, WingStop has grown to over 1,500 locations worldwide. Known for their cooked-to-order wings and unique flavor profiles, WingStop has become a favorite destination for wing lovers across the United States.

Wingstop officially offers 12 core signature flavors. Plain is a no-sauce preparation, while limited-time flavors are separate and may not be available at every store. The number of flavors allowed in one order depends on its size and the selected location's live ordering menu.

Bone‑in wings (without breading) and several sauces are gluten‑free. However, cross‑contact can occur in shared fryers. Veggie sticks and some dips are also safe options.
