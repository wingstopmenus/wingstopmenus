What Comes in Wingstop Veggie Sticks?

Official Wingstop pages describe fresh carrot and celery sticks. Regular lists $1.79 and 25 calories; Large lists $3.49 and 50 calories in the supplied August 2026 snapshot.

Can You Choose Only Carrots or Celery?

Yes, where the store supports the option. Wingstop says Veggie Sticks can be customized as a mixed order, carrots only or celery only. Availability can depend on store inventory.

Does a Dip Come With Them?

No. Official product pages say a dip is not included, though one can be added separately or may come with a combo. Ranch, Blue Cheese or another dip adds its own price and calories.

What Does Large Add?

Large doubles the listed calories to 50 and costs $1.70 more. Two Regular orders cost $3.58, nine cents more than Large, with the same displayed calorie total.

When Are Veggie Sticks Useful?

They provide a fresh, crisp side and the lowest listed calorie total among the sides in this batch. They can also replace fries in some combo flows, subject to the restaurant.

Allergen and Handling Note

Plain carrot and celery sticks do not carry the same sauce ingredients as loaded fries, but restaurant handling can still create cross-contact. Celery itself is a declared allergen in some countries. Check Wingstop’s current allergen information for your market.
