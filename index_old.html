Nutritional Information

Making informed choices about your meal is important. Here's what you should know:

High Calories

Wingstop items are high in calories due to deep-frying and sauces.

High Fat Content

Wings are deep-fried in oil, making them a source of unhealthy fats.

Sodium Content

Many ingredients are high in sodium - important for those watching salt intake.

Healthier Options: Choose grilled over fried when available, opt for lighter sauces, and pair with veggie sticks instead of fries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do they have any vegetarian options?

Yes, Wingstop offers limited vegetarian options including veggie sticks, buffalo ranch fries, cajun fried corn, and seasoned fries. However, due to cross-contamination in the kitchen, they cannot guarantee 100% vegan food.

What's the cheapest thing on the Wingstop menu?

Veggie sticks are the most affordable food option. Dips and sauces range from $1-$3, and drinks/desserts also offer budget-friendly choices starting around $2.

What kinds of wings does Wingstop offer?

Wingstop offers three main types: boneless wings, bone-in (classic) wings, and crispy tenders. All come in 14 different flavors including Lemon Pepper, Garlic Parmesan, Original Hot, Atomic, and more.

Do they have any meal deals?

Yes! Wingstop offers numerous meal deals including combos, family packs, party packs, and special bundles. These range from meals for 2 people to group packs serving large parties with 100+ wings.

Are Wingstop wings healthy?

Wingstop wings are high in calories, fat, and sodium due to deep-frying and sauces. For healthier options, choose lighter flavors, skip the fries, and opt for veggie sticks as your side.

What are the most popular flavors?

The most popular flavors are Lemon Pepper, Garlic Parmesan, Original Hot, Louisiana Rub, and Atomic. Each offers a unique taste profile from mild to extremely spicy.
