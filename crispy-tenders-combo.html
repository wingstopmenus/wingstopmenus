Choosing one flavor

Unlike the 6-piece and 10-piece Classic Combo listings, the official three-tender combo specifies one flavor. Pick something you will enjoy across all three strips, rather than the hottest option merely for novelty.

  • Low-heat savory: Garlic Parmesan or Lemon Pepper.
  • Sweet and smoky: Hickory Smoked BBQ.
  • Traditional wing flavor: Mild or Original Hot.
  • Sweet heat: Mango Habanero if you already enjoy noticeable spice.

See the flavor comparison for more detail. Promotions and limited-time flavors can differ by store.

How tenders eat differently from boneless wings

Tenders are longer strips of white-meat chicken, while boneless wings are smaller breaded pieces. Three tenders can feel more substantial than a three-piece count suggests. Their larger surface also makes the selected sauce prominent in every bite.

Side and dip strategy

Fries make the order more indulgent and add another seasoned, fried component. Veggie sticks bring temperature and texture contrast. A creamy dip can soften a hot sauce, but it also changes the nutrition and allergen profile.

Useful pairing: With a rich flavor such as Garlic Parmesan, veggie sticks keep the meal from feeling repetitive. With a hot flavor, save the dip for the tenders and use it gradually.

Allergens and shared cooking oil

Check breading, sauce, dip and cross-contact together. Wingstop’s official tender page says all food is cooked in shared oil containing soy ingredients and notes that nutrition can vary with preparation and customization. Breaded crispy chicken should not be treated as gluten-free. Review the allergen guide and speak directly with the restaurant for serious allergies.

Frequently asked questions

What comes in the 3 Pc Crispy Tender Combo?

Three crispy tenders in one flavor, regular fries or veggie sticks, one dip and a 20-ounce drink, based on the current official listing reviewed.

Are the tenders sauced?

Wingstop describes them as cooked to order and hand-sauced in the selected flavor.

Can I choose two flavors?

The official three-piece listing specifies one flavor. Check your selected store in case its options differ.

Are crispy tenders gluten-free?

Do not treat breaded crispy tenders as gluten-free. Shared cooking oil and preparation also raise cross-contact concerns.
