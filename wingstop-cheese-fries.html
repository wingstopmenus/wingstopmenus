What Are Wingstop Cheese Fries?

Official Wingstop pages describe Cheese Fries as crispy Seasoned Fries topped with warm jalapeño cheese. The August 2026 snapshot lists Regular at $3.99 and 580 calories and Large at $5.49 and 1,020 calories.

What Does the Cheese Add?

Compared with plain Seasoned Fries, the listed topping adds 80 calories to Regular and 120 calories to Large. The comparison uses the site’s current menu cards; preparation and portioning can vary.

Which Size Offers More?

Large costs $1.50 more than Regular and lists 440 more calories. Two Regular Cheese Fries cost $7.98 and list 1,160 calories, so one Large costs less but also carries a smaller listed total.

Can Cheese Fries Replace a Combo Side?

Official location pages say Cheese Fries can be ordered as an added side or used as a combo upgrade. The upgrade charge appears only after selecting a restaurant.

Can They Be Customized?

Wingstop says customers can modify the fry seasoning. Cheese quantity and removal controls depend on the live cart. Check the final build before using the displayed calorie number.

Allergens and Shared Oil

Cheese sauce introduces dairy concerns, and fries use shared fryers. Review the current Wingstop allergen information when milk, soy, wheat or another allergen affects the order.
