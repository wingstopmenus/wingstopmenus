What Comes on Wingstop Cheese Fries?

Wingstop Cheese Fries start with Seasoned Fries and add warm jalapeño cheese sauce. The September 2026 snapshot lists Regular at $3.99 with 580 calories and Large at $5.49 with 1,020 calories. Prices and availability can change by restaurant and ordering channel.

What Does “Jalapeño Cheese” Mean Here?

Wingstop tops these fries with warm jalapeño cheese sauce. The topping is a creamy cheese sauce with mild pepper character, not sliced jalapeños scattered over the fries. You can order Cheese Fries alone, add them as a side, or choose them as a combo-side upgrade when your selected store offers it.

Because sauce coverage affects texture, customers who expect a very crisp fry may prefer Seasoned Fries with cheese sauce ordered separately, if the selected store offers that customization.

Regular or Large: Which Size Makes Sense?

Moving from Regular to Large adds $1.50 and 440 listed calories. Regular suits one diner or a combo upgrade. Large makes more sense for two people sharing, especially when the cheese fries replace another side rather than accompany several sides.

Can You Customize Cheese Fries?

You can adjust the fry seasoning, while some stores also provide topping or sauce controls in the cart. Options vary, so do not assume cheese on the side, extra cheese, or removed seasoning is available everywhere. Customizations can also change the nutrition total displayed for the standard build.

Allergen and Cross-Contact Notes

Cheese sauce makes dairy a central concern, and the fries are cooked in shared oil. Wingstop states that shared oil contains soy ingredients and that cross-contact cannot be guaranteed. Check the allergen details for the complete order, then confirm the exact build with the restaurant if you have a food allergy. “No cheese” turns this into a different order and may still leave cross-contact risk.

Common Questions About Cheese Fries

Wingstop describes the topping as warm jalapeño cheese sauce over Seasoned Fries. It is a sauce, not a topping of sliced jalapeños.

Cheese Fries use the same seasoned-fry base and add jalapeño cheese sauce. In the snapshot, that adds $1 and 80 listed calories to Regular or 120 to Large.
