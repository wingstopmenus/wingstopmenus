How to Order Online

Follow these simple steps to order from WingStop:

  1. Visit the official WingStop website or download the WingStop mobile app
  2. Select your location to find the nearest store
  3. Click on "Order Now" or "Start Order"
  4. Select your favorite menu items or customize your order
  5. Add items to your cart and review
  6. Choose pickup or delivery option
  7. Enter payment information and confirm your order
  8. Pro Tip: Sign up for WingStop Rewards to earn points toward free food and receive exclusive offers!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do they have vegetarian options? +

Yes, WingStop offers limited vegetarian options including veggie sticks, fries, buffalo ranch fries, cajun fried corn, and seasoned fries. However, due to shared cooking equipment, cross-contamination with chicken products may occur. Vegan options are extremely limited.

What's the cheapest thing on the menu? +

Veggie sticks are the most affordable option at $1.79 for regular and $3.49 for large. Dips and sauces range from $1.49-$4.69, offering budget-friendly additions to any order. Single wings start around $1.79-$1.99 each depending on type.

What kinds of wings does WingStop offer? +

WingStop offers three main types: Bone-in classic wings, boneless wings, and crispy tenders. All come in 14 different flavors ranging from mild to atomic spice levels. They also offer "Thigh Bites" at select locations.

Do they have meal deals? +

Yes! WingStop offers boneless meal deals, all-in bundles, combo deals, and family/group packs. Special meals for 2 are also available, ranging from $16.99 to $31.29. Check the app for exclusive online-only deals.

What is the most popular flavor? +

Classic bone-in wings remain the most popular, followed by Lemon Pepper, Atomic, and Hawaiian flavors. Garlic Parmesan is favorite for those who prefer milder, savory tastes. Mango Habanero has gained significant popularity in recent years.

Can I customize my order? +

Absolutely! You can choose your preferred wing type, select multiple flavors per order, choose your dip, and customize sides. Most combo meals are fully customizable. You can also request extra crispy or well-done wings.

Are prices consistent across all locations? +

Prices may vary by location, region, and market conditions. The prices listed here represent average USD pricing. Airport, stadium, and international locations typically have higher prices. Always check your local WingStop for exact pricing.

How often is the menu updated? +

Our WingStop menu and pricing database is updated regularly (Last Updated: August 2026). WingStop occasionally introduces limited-time offerings (LTOs) and seasonal flavors. Prices and availability may change. Contact your local restaurant for the most current information.

Is there a student or senior discount? +

Discount policies vary by location. Some franchises may offer special discounts. The best way to save is through the WingStop Rewards program, which offers points on every purchase that can be redeemed for free food. Check with your nearest WingStop for local promotions.

Do they offer catering? +

Many WingStop locations offer catering services including party platters, group packs, and catering options. Some locations offer "WingStop At Home" kits. Contact your local restaurant directly or check online for catering menus and minimum order requirements.

Does WingStop have a loyalty program? +

Yes! WingStop Rewards lets you earn 10 points for every $1 spent. Points can be redeemed for free menu items. Members also get exclusive offers, birthday rewards, and early access to new flavors. Sign up through the website or mobile app.

What delivery services does WingStop use? +

WingStop partners with multiple delivery services including DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and Postmates. You can also order directly through the WingStop website or app for delivery. Delivery fees and minimums vary by location and platform.

Are there any allergen concerns? +

WingStop items contain common allergens including wheat, soy, milk, and eggs. The fryers are used for multiple products, so cross-contamination is possible. For specific allergen information, consult the official nutritional guide or contact the restaurant directly.
