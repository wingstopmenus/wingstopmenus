Q: Does Wingstop have gluten-free options?
A: Yes, Wingstop offers gluten-free options:
  • Bone-in and boneless wings are naturally gluten-free
  • Sauces vary in gluten content - ask your local store
  • Fries are typically gluten-free but use shared fryers
  • Cross-contamination is a risk - always notify staff of allergies

Q: What's the difference between bone-in and boneless wings?
A: Key differences:
  • Bone-in: More meat, richer flavor, higher protein (~50g/10pc), more authentic "wing" taste
  • Boneless: Breaded chicken tenders, softer, easier to eat, less protein (~30g/10pc), more carbs from breading
  • Both available in: All Wingstop flavors
  • Price difference: Boneless typically $1-$2 less per order
Choose based on your preference for crunch vs. tenderness!
