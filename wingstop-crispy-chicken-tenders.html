What Should You Know Before Ordering?

Wingstop crispy tenders are breaded chicken strips that can be ordered à la carte or as a combo. Flavor, piece count, dip, side, and drink choices determine the final meal.

What Comes With Wingstop Crispy Tenders?

À la carte tenders focus on the chicken and selected flavor. A tenders combo adds a regular side, dip, and drink according to the current store menu. Read the item description because piece counts and meal bundles differ.

How Do Tenders Compare With Boneless Wings?

Tenders are longer breaded strips; boneless wings are smaller breaded chicken pieces. Both can carry Wingstop flavors, but the shape, portioning, and menu sizes are different.

Calories and Allergens

There is no single tender-combo calorie number. Piece count, flavor, dip, fries, drink, and substitutions all change the total.

Tenders are breaded and should not be treated as a gluten-free choice. Review the allergen guide and current restaurant information for ingredients and shared-preparation cautions.

Ordering Checklist

  • Choose the piece count before comparing prices.
  • Pick the flavor and dip separately.
  • Check whether the combo or à la carte order fits your appetite.
  • Confirm side upgrades and the final cart total.
