⚡ Quick Answer: What Was Wicked Punch?

Wingstop's Sour Patch Kids Wicked Punch was a limited-time glaze: a sour-then-sweet redberry sauce finished with Sour Patch Kids Bitz candy pieces. It ran nationwide from October 27 to November 5, 2026, and it is now gone.

🍬 What Was the Sour Patch Kids Wicked Punch Flavor?

Wingstop built Wicked Punch around a simple idea: candy on chicken. The glaze starts sour, turns sweet, and carries a redberry flavor under a finish of Sour Patch Kids Bitz — small crunchy candy pieces scattered over the sauce. Wingstop billed it as "Halloween's most unhinged flavor," and the launch tied into Sour Patch Kids' "13 Days of Mischief" campaign, which ran October 19–31.

Despite the name, the flavor was not hot. The Takeout's review called it "sweet and not at all spicy," comparing it to orange chicken with a berry flavor. Wingstop never published a heat rating for it, so any heat score you see elsewhere is a guess, not an official number.

The candy finish was the talking point. Most Wingstop flavors are a rub or a sauce; this one added a crunchy, sweet-tart topping that changed the texture as much as the taste. That is also why it split opinion: reviewers who like sweet glazes rated it well, while fans of dry heat found it closer to dessert than to wings.

📅 When Did Wicked Punch Run?

Wingstop announced the flavor on October 13, 2026. Club Wingstop members got it first, from October 23, and it went nationwide on October 27 at participating U.S. locations. It disappeared after November 5, 2026, while supplies lasted.

The end date caused some confusion online, because Wingstop's other fall limited-time flavor, the Lemon Pepper Trio, runs until November 11. The two windows are different: Wicked Punch ended November 5, the Trio runs to November 11.

If a location still shows it in the app after November 5, that is leftover stock, not a new run. Wingstop has not announced a return date, and limited-time flavors on this site have not come back once their window closed.

💰 Formats and Prices

Wicked Punch came on every protein Wingstop sells: bone-in wings, boneless wings, and crispy tenders. The most common promo items were a 5-piece Wicked Punch wings order and a 2-piece Wicked Punch crispy tenders order.

Wingstop never set one nationwide price for it. What you paid depended on the store, the protein, and the order size. Prices seen at U.S. locations during the run:

⭐ What Reviewers Said

The Takeout tried it across all three proteins and landed on sweet, not spicy: the glaze reads like orange chicken with a berry edge, and the candy Bitz add crunch rather than heat. That matches the flavor's design — it was built as a Halloween candy stunt, not as a heat challenge.

Coverage agreed on the basics: announced October 13, early access for Club members on October 23, nationwide October 27, gone after November 5. Several outlets noted the same thing buyers did — the price moved by store, so there was no single number to quote.

The verdict that stuck was about the idea itself. A candy glaze on wings sounds strange until you try it, and the reviewers who did mostly called it fun rather than a gimmick. It was never meant to be a permanent flavor, and Wingstop treated it that way: a short Halloween run, then gone.

🔥 What to Order Now Instead

The closest thing still on the menu is the Lemon Pepper Trio, which runs to November 11, 2026: Original Lemon Pepper plus two new twists, Chili Crunch and Chili Glaze. It is the other fall limited-time flavor, and it is still live while Wicked Punch is not.

If you liked the sweet side of Wicked Punch, try the Hawaiian or Mango Habanero flavors on the regular menu, or check the deals page for the current bundles. The full flavor list is on the flavors page, and the full menu has current prices by item.
