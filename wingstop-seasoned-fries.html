What Comes on Wingstop Seasoned Fries?

Wingstop describes its fries as cooked to order, crisp outside, fluffy inside and coated with signature Fry Seasoning. Our August 2026 snapshot lists Regular at $2.99 and 500 calories and Large at $4.49 and 900 calories.

How Much Larger Is the Large Order?

Large costs $1.50 more and lists 400 additional calories, an 80% increase over Regular. The card does not state ounces, so calories provide a useful comparison but not an exact weight.

Can You Customize the Seasoning?

Official Wingstop location pages say customers can adjust the amount and type of seasoning whether fries are ordered alone or inside a combo. Available controls depend on the selected store’s cart.

Which Size Fits the Order?

Regular works for one person or as a combo side. Large fits sharing or a group meal. Two Regular orders cost $5.98 and list 1,000 calories, while one Large costs $4.49 and lists 900 calories in this snapshot.

Pickup and Delivery

Fresh fries lose crispness as steam collects in closed packaging. Shorter travel time usually protects texture better. Reheating changes texture and does not change the listed nutrition.

Shared Fryer Warning

Official location pages say fries are considered gluten-free by ingredients but are cooked in shared fryers. Wingstop also warns that shared oil contains soy ingredients and cross-contact cannot be guaranteed. Review the current allergen guidance.
