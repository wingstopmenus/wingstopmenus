What Cheese Sauce Sizes Does Wingstop List?

Our August 2026 menu snapshot lists three sizes: Regular for $1.89 and 120 calories, Medium for $2.89 and 230 calories, and Large for $4.69 and 620 calories. All three sizes belong to the same Cheese Sauce item.

How Does Wingstop Use Cheese Sauce?

Official Wingstop location pages describe Louisiana Voodoo Fries as Cajun-seasoned fries drizzled with Cheese Sauce and House Made Ranch. The separate Cheese Sauce cup lets you add more or use it with another side. Adding a cup changes the loaded fries’ calorie total.

Which Size Fits One Order?

Regular is enough for a light drizzle or an individual side. Medium nearly doubles the Regular calorie amount and costs one dollar more. Large is designed for a group or several items; it lists more than five Regular calorie portions.

Which Size Gives More Sauce for the Price?

Calories provide a rough portion comparison when the recipe is the same. Medium costs about 53% more than Regular and lists about 92% more calories. Large costs about 148% more than Regular and lists about 417% more calories. This suggests better quantity value at Large, but only if the order uses it.

Can You Treat Calories as Exact Cup Volume?

No. Calories indicate energy, not fluid ounces. Recipe fill, serving definitions and preparation can affect the figures. Wingstop does not show cup ounces on the supplied menu cards, so avoid inventing size measurements.

Is Cheese Sauce Included on Loaded Fries?

Cheese Sauce is part of the published Louisiana Voodoo Fries build. A separate sauce cup is an extra item unless the live cart says otherwise. Other fry customizations and availability depend on the restaurant.

Allergens and Preparation

Cheese Sauce should be treated as a dairy-containing product unless current official information confirms otherwise. Review Wingstop’s allergen guidance and ask the restaurant about milk, fish or other relevant allergens. Wingstop warns that cross-contact cannot be guaranteed.
