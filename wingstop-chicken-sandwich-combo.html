What Comes in the Chicken Sandwich Combo?

The current official Wingstop combo page lists one cooked-to-order Chicken Sandwich, seasoned fries, a side of ranch, and a drink. The sandwich uses a hand-sauced crispy filet on a toasted bun with pickles.

How Is the Combo Different From the À-La-Carte Sandwich?

The à-la-carte Chicken Sandwich stops at the sandwich. The combo adds three separate items: fries, ranch, and a drink. That difference affects price, calories, allergens, and how filling the order is.

Choose à la carte when

You already have sides, do not want a fountain drink, or are adding the sandwich to a shared order.

Choose the combo when

You want the standard fries, ranch, and drink without adding each one separately.

How Should You Choose a Sandwich Flavor?

Flavor choice affects both heat and texture. Wet sauces fully coat the breaded filet; dry seasonings add flavor with less surface moisture. On a delivery order, that difference can matter because the sandwich remains enclosed while traveling.

Wingstop’s official pages currently describe 12 sandwich flavors and name Lemon Pepper and Garlic Parmesan as examples. Confirm the current list after selecting your restaurant because limited-time flavors rotate.

How Many Calories Are in the Full Combo?

The total is a sum, not one universal number: sandwich plus selected flavor, fries, ranch, and the exact drink. A zero-sugar beverage and a regular soda do not have the same total. Side upgrades also change the calculation.

  1. Start with the flavored sandwich.
  2. Add the seasoned-fries portion.
  3. Add the amount of ranch you expect to use.
  4. Add the exact beverage and size.

Use the current nutrition guide for each component. This avoids treating the broad menu estimate as though every customization were identical.

How Much Does the Combo Cost?

Our August 2026 menu snapshot shows an observed price of $8.29. Prices are store-specific. Before calling it a value, compare that live combo price with the same store’s à-la-carte sandwich, side, dip, and drink prices.

Can You Upgrade the Side?

Some ordering menus may offer loaded fries or another side for an extra charge. That is a store/cart option, not a guaranteed national inclusion. Check the price change and remember that cheese sauce, ranch, or other toppings also change nutrition and allergens.

Delivery and Pickup Considerations

The fries and sauced filet both lose crispness as steam builds in closed packaging. A shorter pickup trip usually protects texture better than a long delivery. If travel time is unavoidable, a dry-seasoned sandwich is the more practical option for retaining surface crunch.

Allergens and Shared Preparation

The sandwich is breaded and contains wheat; ranch introduces its own allergen considerations. Wingstop states that food is cooked in shared oil containing soy ingredients. Review the allergen guide and ask the restaurant about the exact substitution when an allergy affects your order.

Combo Ordering Checklist

  • Confirm all four standard components appear in the cart.
  • Select the sandwich flavor.
  • Choose the exact drink instead of relying on a calorie range.
  • Review any side-upgrade charge.
  • Compare pickup and delivery totals before checkout.
