What Comes on Louisiana Voodoo Fries?

Wingstop’s official product pages describe fries tossed in Cajun seasoning, then drizzled with Cheese Sauce and House Made Ranch. Regular lists $4.49 and 680 calories; Large lists $5.99 and 1,080 calories in our August 2026 snapshot.

How Do They Differ From Cheese Fries?

Voodoo Fries add Cajun seasoning and Ranch to the cheese-topped fry base. Regular Voodoo lists 100 more calories than Regular Cheese Fries, while Large lists 60 more than Large Cheese Fries.

Which Size Should You Choose?

Large costs $1.50 more and lists 400 additional calories. Regular fits an individual loaded side; Large makes more sense when sharing. Sauce coverage can soften fries faster than on the plain version.

Can the Toppings Be Changed?

Official pages say the Cajun seasoning, Cheese Sauce and Ranch can be customized. Removing a topping changes the product and means the standard calorie figure may no longer apply.

Are They a Combo Upgrade?

Wingstop says Voodoo Fries can replace a preselected combo side where the store offers an upgrade. The cart determines the charge and availability.

Allergen Warning

Cheese Sauce and Ranch add allergen considerations beyond plain fries, while the fries use shared oil. Check the current official allergen guide and contact the restaurant for the exact build.
