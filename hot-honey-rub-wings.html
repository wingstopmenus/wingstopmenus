Wingstop Hot Honey Rub Wings – sticky, sweet, and spicy dry rub

What Are Wingstop Hot Honey Rub Wings?

Hot Honey Rub Wings feature a dry rub (or sticky glaze, depending on location) made with real honey, cayenne pepper, vinegar, and spices. It delivers a unique sweet‑heat experience – the honey sweetness hits first, followed by a building cayenne warmth. This flavor has become a fan favorite for those who love the contrast of sweet and spicy. Available on classic bone‑in wings, boneless wings, and crispy tenders. Explore all flavors in our complete Wingstop flavors guide.

🍯🌶️ Flavor Profile: Sticky, sweet honey upfront with a gradual cayenne heat. Medium spice level – similar to Original Hot but sweeter.
