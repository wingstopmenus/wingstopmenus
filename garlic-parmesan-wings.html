Wingstop Garlic Parmesan Wings – savory, garlicky dry rub

What Are Wingstop Garlic Parmesan Wings?

Wingstop Garlic Parmesan Wings are a dry rub flavor combining roasted garlic, parmesan cheese, butter, and herbs. It has no heat, making it one of the best mild options on the menu. The seasoning sticks to the wings without making them soggy.

🧄 Quick Taste Summary: Buttery, garlicky, savory, with a subtle parmesan richness. No spice – perfect for those who dislike buffalo heat.

Is Garlic Parmesan Better as Bone-In or Boneless?

Bone‑in Garlic Parmesan wings are best for maximum crispiness. The dry rub adheres perfectly to the skin. Boneless works well too, but the breading softens slightly.

Best Pick: Bone‑in for texture; boneless for convenience.
