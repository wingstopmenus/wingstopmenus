What Comes on Buffalo Ranch Fries?

Wingstop describes Buffalo Ranch Fries as fries loaded with tangy buffalo flavor and drizzled with creamy House Made Ranch. Regular lists $4.49 and 610 calories; Large lists $5.99 and 1,070 calories.

How Are They Different From Voodoo Fries?

Buffalo Ranch uses buffalo sauce and Ranch. Louisiana Voodoo uses Cajun seasoning, Cheese Sauce and Ranch. Regular Buffalo Ranch lists 70 fewer calories than Regular Voodoo, while their Large totals differ by 10 calories.

What Does Large Add?

Large costs $1.50 more and lists 460 more calories, about 75% above Regular. Regular is easier for one person; Large fits sharing or a larger group order.

Can You Adjust the Sauces?

Official pages say the sauces can be adjusted. Less Ranch or buffalo sauce changes taste, moisture and nutrition, so the standard calorie total may not match a customized order.

Delivery Texture

Two wet sauces add moisture. During a longer delivery, fries can soften more quickly than plain Seasoned Fries. Pickup and eating soon after collection usually preserves more texture.

Allergen Warning

House Made Ranch and shared fryer handling require care for customers with allergies. Review Wingstop’s current allergen information and ask the store about the exact sauces.
