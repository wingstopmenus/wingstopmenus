What Does Buffalo Ranch Taste Like?

Buffalo hot sauce contributes vinegar-forward heat, while House Made Ranch adds creaminess and cools the finish. Unlike Louisiana Voodoo Fries, this build does not list jalapeño cheese sauce or Cajun seasoning.

Is Large Worth the Upgrade?

Large adds $1.50 and 460 listed calories. Regular is the more controlled personal portion. Large works for sharing, though sauce-coated fries soften during travel and as they sit. If available, sauce on the side provides more control over heat, ranch coverage and texture.

Customization and Allergen Guidance

You can customize the sauces when the local ordering menu provides that option. Ranch commonly raises milk and egg concerns, and the shared frying oil contains soy ingredients. Cross-contact cannot be guaranteed. Check the allergen details for your complete order and confirm the preparation with the restaurant if an allergy affects your order.
