What Should You Know Before Ordering?

Wingstop drink choices usually include fountain beverages, bottled water, and lemonade, with the exact lineup set by the selected restaurant. Your drink can make a large difference to a combo’s calorie and sugar total.

What Drinks Does Wingstop Sell?

Most U.S. menus show Coca-Cola fountain beverages, bottled water, and lemonade options. Brands, cup sizes, and shareable formats can vary, so select a restaurant before treating any lineup as final.

How Should You Count Drink Calories?

The total depends on beverage, cup size, ice, and refill. A menu range can include both zero-calorie and sugar-sweetened choices, so use the exact beverage rather than applying one number to every fountain drink.

Does a Drink Come With Every Order?

No. Drinks are normally included in eligible combos or sold separately. Wing and tender packs may have different inclusions, so read the cart description before checkout.
