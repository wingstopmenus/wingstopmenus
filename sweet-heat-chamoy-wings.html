What Was Wingstop Sweet Heat Chamoy?

Sweet Heat Chamoy featuring Tajín was Wingstop's summer 2026 limited-time flavor. Wingstop built it in two layers: a custom chamoy dry rub followed by a drizzle of Tajín Chamoy Sauce. The dry seasoning brought fruit sweetness and tang, while the drizzle added chile and citrus notes. Club Wingstop members received early access from June 26 through June 29, and the nationwide launch followed on June 30.

During the promotion, customers could add it to classic or boneless wings, crispy tenders, chicken sandwiches, fries, and Cajun fried corn. Wingstop also introduced Chamoy Ranch as the matching dip. For the core lineup, visit our complete Wingstop flavors guide.
