What Should You Know Before Ordering?

The à-la-carte Wingstop Chicken Sandwich is one crispy, hand-sauced chicken filet on a toasted bun with pickles. Fries, ranch and a drink are not part of the standalone sandwich order.

What Comes on the Wingstop Chicken Sandwich?

The standard sandwich has a crispy chicken filet, pickles and a toasted bun. You choose an available Wingstop flavor for the filet. The official product description identifies the sandwich as cooked to order and hand-sauced.

What Is Not Included?

The à-la-carte listing does not automatically include fries, ranch or a drink. Choose the Chicken Sandwich Combo when you want those items together.

How Do Flavor Choices Affect Calories?

The base sandwich and selected seasoning or sauce determine the sandwich total. Do not apply the combo calorie estimate to this item because the standalone order excludes the side, dip and drink.

How Much Does the Sandwich Cost?

The menu currently shows an observed price of $5.79. Treat it as a reference only; the selected restaurant and ordering channel set the final price.

Allergens and Cross-Contact

The breaded chicken and bun make this unsuitable as a gluten-free selection. Check the allergen guide and ask the restaurant about shared preparation when an allergy affects the order.
