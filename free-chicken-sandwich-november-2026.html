Wingstop Chicken Sandwich — free with any purchase for Club members, Nov 8–9, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer: How Do I Get the Free Sandwich?

Club Wingstop members get a free Chicken Sandwich with any purchase on Sunday, November 8 and Monday, November 9, 2026. Pick any of the 12 signature flavors, with one dipping sauce included. Limit one free sandwich per person, at participating locations, ordered online or in the app.

📌 Key Facts
🗓️ Dates: Sunday, November 8 and Monday, November 9, 2026.
🆓 Offer: one free Chicken Sandwich with any purchase.
👥 Who: Club Wingstop members only. Joining is free.
🍗 Choice: any of the 12 signature flavors, plus one dipping sauce.
📍 Where: participating U.S. locations, online or in the Wingstop app.

🥪 How to Get the Free Chicken Sandwich

The offer runs for two days: Sunday, November 8 and Monday, November 9, 2026. Monday is National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day itself, and Wingstop starts the celebration a day early on Sunday.

Claiming it takes three steps. First, join Club Wingstop if you are not already a member — it is free, and you can sign up in the Wingstop app or on wingstop.com. Second, place any order on November 8 or 9, online or in the app, at a participating location. Third, add the free Chicken Sandwich in your chosen flavor, with one dipping sauce included.

There is no minimum spend stated in the offer. Any purchase qualifies, and the sandwich is the free item. The deal is tied to your Club Wingstop account, so order while signed in.

📋 The Fine Print

Like most two-day giveaways, this one has limits worth knowing before you order:

  • One per person. The free sandwich is limited to one per person across the two days.
  • Club members only. You must be a Club Wingstop (rewards) member. Non-members can join free before ordering.
  • Participating locations. Not every store has to take part. Confirm in the app when you select your restaurant.
  • Online or in the app. The offer is valid through Wingstop's own ordering channels. Third-party delivery orders are excluded.
  • No combining. The free sandwich cannot be combined with other promotions.
  • Dates are fixed. The offer ends November 9, 2026. Deal listings show it expiring that night.

If your store does not show the free sandwich at checkout, it is most likely a participation issue — try another nearby location or ask in store.

🍗 What the Sandwich Is

The free item is Wingstop's regular Chicken Sandwich: a breaded, fried chicken filet cooked to order, tossed in your selected flavor, and served on a toasted bun with pickles. Fries, ranch, and a drink are separate unless you order the combo — the free sandwich is the standalone item.

You can pick any of Wingstop's 12 signature flavors for it, from Lemon Pepper and Garlic Parmesan to Mango Habanero and Original Hot. One dipping sauce comes with it; deal listings name ranch, bleu cheese, cheese sauce, and honey mustard among the choices. Dry seasonings hold their crunch better if you are ordering delivery, while wet sauces coat the breading more heavily.

Full details on the build, calories, and allergens are on the Chicken Sandwich guide. Note that the sandwich contains wheat and is not considered gluten-free.

💰 What It Is Worth

Deal listings quote the sandwich's regular price as $6.99, with the note that the price may vary by location. That is the saving on the free sandwich if your store charges the listed price.

This site's own November 2026 menu snapshot observed a lower à-la-carte price of $5.79 at the stores checked. The two numbers are not a contradiction — Wingstop prices vary by location, and the $6.99 figure is the regular price quoted in the offer listings. Check your store's live menu in the app to see the exact price where you order.

Either way, the sandwich is free with any purchase for two days, so the saving is the full menu price of the sandwich at your location.

🔥 What Else Is On Right Now

The free sandwich is the headline, but it is not the only thing running. The Lemon Pepper Trio — Chili Crunch and Chili Glaze alongside Original Lemon Pepper — is still live until November 11, 2026. The Sour Patch Kids Wicked Punch glaze ended November 5, so it is gone. Wingstop also runs 30 wings for $30 on Thursdays, and the deals page lists the current bundles and Club offers.

If you are ordering on November 8 or 9 anyway, the free sandwich stacks with the trip: order your wings or tenders, add the free sandwich in a different flavor, and you have two flavors for the price of one order.
