❓ WingStop Wings FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

What's the difference between Bone-In and Boneless wings at WingStop? +

Bone-In are traditional chicken wing sections. Boneless are made from all-white meat chicken breast, cut into wing-sized pieces and breaded. Both can be tossed in any sauce.

What is the most popular WingStop flavor? +

According to sales data, Lemon Pepper is the consistent top seller, followed closely by Original Hot and Mango Habanero.

Can I mix flavors in a combo? +

Yes! All combos and group packs allow you to choose 2 or more flavors. This is the best way to try new sauces.

What are the spiciest wings at WingStop? +

The Atomic flavor is officially rated as the hottest (5 flames). Mango Habanero is also very hot but has a sweet start.

How many wings should I order per person? +

For a meal: 6-8 wings per person. For a party: 3-4 wings per person if you have other food. For large groups, use our Group Packs guide above.
