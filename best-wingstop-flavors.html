Best Wingstop Flavor by Order Type

🍗 Classic Wings

Lemon Pepper, Original Hot, Louisiana Rub, and Atomic work well with bone-in wings.

🍖 Boneless Wings

Mango Habanero, Garlic Parmesan, BBQ, and Lemon Pepper coat boneless wings well.

🐓 Tenders

Garlic Parmesan, Lemon Pepper, and Hickory Smoked BBQ are strong tender choices.

🥪 Sandwich

Original Hot, Lemon Pepper, and Mango Habanero are good choices for chicken sandwiches.
