What Are Wingstop Cajun Wings?

Cajun Wings feature a dry rub blend of paprika, garlic, onion, cayenne pepper, and herbs. Unlike wet sauces, this dry rub delivers a concentrated, savory heat that adheres directly to the crispy skin. It's a popular choice for those who want spice without the mess of a wet sauce. Available on classic bone‑in wings, boneless wings, and tenders. Explore all flavors in our complete Wingstop flavors guide.

🌶️ Flavor Profile: Bold, earthy, and moderately spicy. Paprika provides color and sweetness; cayenne adds heat; garlic and onion round it out. A classic Louisiana‑inspired dry rub.

🌶️ Best Cajun Wingstop Combos & Pairings

🧀 Ranch & Celery

The classic pairing for dry rub wings – ranch cools the cayenne and adds creaminess.

🍋 Lemon Pepper Mix

Half Cajun + half Lemon Pepper = spicy, citrusy, and addictive. Best of both rubs.

🧄 Garlic Parmesan

Pair with Garlic Parmesan wet sauce for a spicy‑savory contrast.

🍟 Seasoned Fries

Fries are a great vehicle for extra Cajun seasoning – ask for extra rub.
